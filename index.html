About Lotus Notes

A soft, calm workspace for capturing ideas that matter.

Why this app exists

Lotus Notes was created as a clean, secure place to write and organize your personal thoughts.

How your access works

Authentication is handled through Auth0, so sign-up and login are managed securely before you reach your notes.

What happens after login

You are redirected to your notes dashboard where you can create, view, and delete notes linked to your authenticated account.
